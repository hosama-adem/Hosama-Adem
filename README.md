### 👨‍💻 Executive Summary

I am a **Software Systems Engineer** and **A2SV (Africa to Silicon Valley) Fellow** based at Adama Science and Technology University (ASTU). I specialize in designing and engineering **fault-tolerant distributed backends**, **high-concurrency network services in Go**, and **production-grade enterprise AI/RAG architectures**.

- ⚙️ **Distributed Backends & Concurrency**: Architecting thread-safe reverse proxies, load balancers, and resilient REST/gRPC APIs utilizing Uncle Bob's **Clean Architecture**, Domain-Driven Design (DDD), and concurrency primitives (goroutines, channels, mutexes).
- 🧠 **Production AI & Retrieval Systems**: Designing sub-200ms latency vector retrieval engines, multi-agent evaluation frameworks, and context-augmented pipelines with **LangChain**, **LangGraph**, **FAISS**, and **Sentence Transformers**.
- 🏆 **Algorithmic Rigor**: **ETCPC 2025 Honorable Mention** with **700+ solved algorithmic challenges** across Codeforces, LeetCode, and HackerRank with an emphasis on graph theory, dynamic programming, and computational complexity.
- 🚀 **Impact & Leadership**: Core Architect @ **SRAHub** and Founder of **Tech With Hos**, mentoring 1,000+ developers in systems architecture, concurrent programming, and software engineering craftsmanship.
